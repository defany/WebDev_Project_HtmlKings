Who Am I?

Hey! I'm an anime enthusiast and web designer currently living in Japan. I created Anime Heaven as a cozy corner for everyone who loves anime art, stories, and culture. My goal is to bring fans together and celebrate the creativity and emotion behind every work.

The Site Idea

Anime Heaven was born from the idea of building a warm, welcoming place for anime lovers. A place where people can discover new art, share their feelings, and explore the stories that inspire us. The site mixes clean UI, smooth animations, and a cozy atmosphere inspired by Japanese pop culture.

My Favorite Anime

One Piece

"One Piece" has been my all-time favorite since childhood. It's not just pirates and treasure — it's freedom, friendship, dreams, and the unbreakable bonds between the Straw Hats.
